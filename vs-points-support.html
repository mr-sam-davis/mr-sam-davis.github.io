Support

VS Points — the app that appears as Points on your home screen — is made by one person, so support comes straight from me. Email sam@samapps.co.uk and I'll get back to you as soon as I reasonably can.

It helps enormously if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and — where it applies — the route and dates you were looking at when something went wrong.

Common questions

The seats shown don't match Virgin Atlantic's website

Reward availability changes constantly, and the app works from data gathered periodically rather than live from Virgin Atlantic. Always confirm on virginatlantic.com before booking. If the difference looks persistent rather than momentary, please tell me — that's a bug worth knowing about.

How do I cancel Points Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app. Open the account screen in VS Points and choose to manage your subscription, or go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

If you're on the 12-month commitment plan, cancelling stops it renewing into another 12-month term, but the term you're currently in continues to its end.

I've paid but Points Plus isn't unlocked

Open the subscription screen in the app and choose Restore Purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with. If that doesn't do it, email me.

I'm not receiving price notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for VS Points in Settings → Notifications, that the alert still exists in the app, and that the flights it watches haven't already departed — alerts expire automatically once they have. Price notifications require an active Points Plus subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't issue them directly, but do tell me what went wrong.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything held on your device. Deleting a price alert removes its record from our database. If you'd like anything else removed, email me and I'll take care of it — see the privacy policy for what is held and why.

Can you add another airline?

Not at the moment — VS Points is built specifically around Virgin Atlantic reward availability. Suggestions are always welcome, though.

Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Both go to the same place: sam@samapps.co.uk. Feature requests genuinely do shape what gets built next.
