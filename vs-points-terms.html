What the app does

VS Points shows reward seat availability and points prices for Virgin Atlantic flights, gathered from publicly available sources. It is a search and monitoring tool. It does not sell flights, hold reservations, or act as a travel agent, and it has no access to your Flying Club account.

Accuracy

Availability and pricing change constantly, and the information in the app may be out of date, incomplete, or wrong. It is offered for guidance only.

Round trip prices are a particular case: the app adds together two one-way reward fares, and Virgin Atlantic prices seats differently when they are booked as a round trip, so the real price may be higher or lower than the total shown.

Always confirm availability and price with Virgin Atlantic before making any booking or travel decision. We are not responsible for bookings made, missed, or mispriced on the basis of what the app showed.
