What the app does

VS Points shows reward seat availability and points prices for Virgin Atlantic flights, gathered from publicly available sources. It is a search and monitoring tool. It does not sell flights, hold reservations, or act as a travel agent, and it has no access to your Flying Club account.

Accuracy

Availability and pricing change constantly, and the information in the app may be out of date, incomplete, or wrong. It is offered for guidance only.

Round trip prices are a particular case: the app adds together two one-way reward fares, and Virgin Atlantic prices seats differently when they are booked as a round trip, so the real price may be higher or lower than the total shown.

Always confirm availability and price with Virgin Atlantic before making any booking or travel decision. We are not responsible for bookings made, missed, or mispriced on the basis of what the app showed.

Prices in the app are also not live. Routes and dates are refreshed on a rolling schedule rather than checked the moment you look, so what you see can be up to around a day old, and a change that appears and disappears between refreshes may never show up at all.

About notifications

Notifications are sent on a best-effort basis. Each flight's price is rechecked roughly once a day, and a separate pass runs every hour to compare those prices against your alerts. So a notification normally reaches you within an hour of the recheck that spotted a change — but that can be up to a day after the change itself. A change that appears and disappears between rechecks may not trigger a notification at all, and occasionally one will be lost in delivery and not sent again. Delivery also depends on Apple's push notification service, your notification settings, and your network.

Please don't treat a notification as your only way of catching a price or a seat. If a trip matters, check the app, and check with Virgin Atlantic.
